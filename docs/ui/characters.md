# Characters and the shared character sheet

Part of the UI docs: [`docs/ui.md`](../ui.md) has the hard rules, tokens, components and the dependency rules every screen follows.

The Characters screen has two routes: `#/characters` is the roster (one 48 px row per character: the name, with a warning badge beside it ("2 low", its sentence "2 worn pieces are low on durability" as the badge's accessible name and tooltip) when any worn piece is low on durability, so the warning stays in the sticky Character column however far the table scrolls; last scan, STR · DEX · INT, Hits · Stam · Mana, the five paperdoll resists with a meter to the cap, pieces worn, "Build suit" and a ⋯ menu), filtered by the top-bar search and sorted by its header buttons; `#/characters/<Name>` is that character's sheet, with a breadcrumb, previous/next, "Show <Name>'s items", "Build a suit" and a ⋯ menu, then one summary line (`roster.mts`'s `sheetMeta`: "Scanned 7 h ago · 11 pieces worn · opened 37 containers", the last part a link left out at none, no serials). `parseRoute()` returns the sheet's name as `sheet` (the builder's `character` is untouched). The link goes to `#/containers/<Name>`, the Containers view listing only the root containers that character's scans opened, their backpack and bank aside (`openedRoots`, the same list the line counts; `parseRoute()`'s `scanner`), with a Show all link in its footer. The ⋯ menus are `menu(anchor, items, {label})` from `components.mts`: a popover with `role=menu`, ↑/↓/Home/End between the items, a danger item for Forget (which goes through `confirmDialog`). "Show <Name>'s items" opens the Inventory with its Character filter set to that character (`inventory.mts`'s `showCharacterItems`).

The sheet itself is one themed component, `sheetNode(name, before, after, opts?)` in `ui/sheet.mts`, used by the Characters screen and the Suit Builder result:

- `before`: the suit to show, a `SheetAssignment` (`Record<string, SheetItem>` keyed by slot or serial; a `SheetItem` is `{serial, name, slot, props, rarity?, tags?, extras?}`, which both a scanned `Item` and an optimizer `OptItem` satisfy). `wornSet(name)` gives the character's worn set in this shape.
- `after`: `null` for the one-suit sheet; a second assignment for the "now → after" variant, where every figure that moves reads "18 → 22" (the after number in success or danger color), the slot tiles show the `after` suit with its new pieces badged "New", and the footnote adds the Hits/Stamina/Mana estimate note. Worn pieces `before` leaves out (those a run saved with twelve slots never planned, or no slot holds) count on both sides; a piece in `before` counts once.
- `opts.onSlot(item, tile)`: called when a filled slot tile is pressed; with or without it the tiles carry the page's item tooltip (`itemTip`), on hover and keyboard focus. The Characters screen opens the piece's tooltip (the same `tipNode` as the hover tooltip) in an always-dark popover (`.item-pop`), with "Open in Inventory", which shows the piece in the Inventory's item peek (`inventory.mts`'s `showItem`); the hover tooltip stays away from the tile while it is open.

It returns a `div.sheet` (with `data-character`): a KPI row of seven tiles (five resists as value / cap with a meter and a "cap +N" badge when the raw sum passes the cap, then Attributes with the gear bonus split and Pools), then two cards in a 7fr / 5fr grid: Worn gear (Armor 3 × 2, Weapons and jewelry 3 × 2, Clothing 3 × 2 plus a Kilt / Skirt tile only while one is worn (issue #202), anything else under Other; rarity-colored borders, dashed empty slots) and Properties (the chosen properties in Casting, Combat, Leech, Regeneration, Pools and other and Other as key/value lists with the shard cap muted; the card's "Properties shown" popover picks them from every property the tooltip reader knows, saved as the ui-prefs `sheetProps`, default `SHEET_GROUPS`; the skills or an info message when the scan has none, and the footnote: the resist cap, the Resisting Spells minimum, and each resist the minimum holds up, "Fire 44: the Resisting Spells minimum."). The sheet lays itself out from its own width with container queries (`ui/characters.css`): below 1040 px the attributes and pools drop under the resists, below 960 px the cards stack, so it fits whatever column it is put in. `resistFigures(name, set)` is the resist maths the roster shares with it. The one-suit sheet badges a worn piece that is low on durability ("Low durability 12/255": at 20% of its max or 10 points, `lowDurability`; a max of 0, no durability line, or a piece at or past its max is never low) on its slot tile, and the Characters sheet page puts a one-line warning under the meta line ("2 worn pieces are low on durability"); nothing shows when nothing is low, and the now → after sheet shows no badge, since an optimizer piece carries no durability. The pure formatters (`capBadgeText`, `atCap`, `bonusBreakdown`, `moveText`, `keyNumbers`, `tagTone`, `plural`, `lowDurability`, `lowDurabilityCount`, `lowDurabilitySummary`) are exported and unit-tested in `app/ui-characters.test.mts`.
