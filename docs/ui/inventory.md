# The Inventory screen

Part of the UI docs: [`docs/ui.md`](../ui.md) has the hard rules, tokens, components and the dependency rules every screen follows.

`ui/inventory.mts` draws the Items view as one instance of the item browser, `ui/item-browser.mts` (issue #12): `createItemBrowser(root, opts)` builds the toolbar, strip, columns popover and virtual table inside `root` and owns its query, loaded page, columns and timers, so it can be mounted more than once. Its options: `fixed` (a partial query always applied and never shown as a chip, token or "+ Filter" entry), `columns` (the default set, `DEFAULT_COLS` when left out), `extraColumn` (one more cell per list row, after Name), `onActivate` (a list row's click or Enter), `rowActions`, `detail` (the peek's hooks), `persist` (save columns, widths and density to ui-prefs) and `globalIds` (the Inventory's `#f-text`, `#inv-settings`, … ids, which its CSS and the Electron tests select; a second instance leaves them off). "/" focuses the search of the browser on screen, unless it was pressed inside another browser. The pure rules (the query string, the active-filter tokens and their wording, the counts and plurals, the empty-result sentence, the row window and the keyboard model) are in `ui/inv-model.mts`, tested under plain `node:test` by `app/ui-inventory.test.mts`.

- **Filter state** is the browser's `query`, the shape `item-query.mts`'s `parseItemQuery` reads. Character, Slot, Location (as bag texts `loc` and whole root containers `root`), Kind and Weapon skill are any-of lists (issue #188: `wskill=swordsmanship`, lower-cased skill names; the facets' `weaponSkills` counts each from `vault-lib.mts`'s `weaponSkillsOf`, where a Use Best Weapon Skill weapon also counts under Swordsmanship, Fencing and Mace Fighting, which the chip's popover says; tokens read "Weapon skill: Swordsmanship"); Rarity is a range (`rarityMin`, `rarityMax`; an item with no tier counts as below every tier); property rules carry an operator (`prop=lmc:le:4`) and can name a numeric tooltip line no property models (issue #133: the facets' `extraKeys`, such as `prop=splintering weapon:10`); a yes/no property is required with `flag` (issue #182: `flag=spell channeling`, repeated, every one must be present), offered from the facets' `flagKeys` as the property rule's last group, **Yes or no** (each option's muted second line reads "Yes / no"), where picking one hides the comparison and the number, moves the focus to Add rule, and shows as a Title Case token ("Spell Channeling"); `vault-lib.mts`'s `flagKeys` says which tooltip flags count (a known yes/no property such as "(imbued)", read Imbued, or a plain-words line on gear; never a slayer, race lock, free text or a resource's material), and its `flagKey` folds every "part of an armor set (N pieces)" into one Part of an Armor Set and lower-cases, for the facet and for matching alike; tags are hidden (`hide`) or required, any of them (`tag`), from the one Tags… popover. Every control goes through `setQuery()`, which redraws the chips (in place, so an open popover keeps its anchor) and the active strip, and refetches.
- **Slayer filter** (issue #189, "+ Filter" › Slayer…) is a single-choice radio list: "No slayer filter", "Any slayer", then the slayers laid out like the shard's slayer charts, from the rules' `slayerGroups` (`inv-model.mts`'s `slayerTree`). Each super slayer ("Reptile Slayer" with a muted **Super** tag and its count) is followed by its lesser slayers one step in ("Dragon Slayer"); a titled group ("Talisman slayers") and then **Other**, A–Z, for any slayer the table lacks, follow; a hairline separates the groups (`slayerTree` marks each group's first row `first`). The "Super" tag is `aria-hidden`, and a heading row has `role="presentation"`. Only slayers the inventory holds are listed: a super slayer it lacks stays as a muted heading with no radio (the arrow keys pass over it) while one of its lesser slayers is there, and an empty group goes. Labels are Title Case plus " Slayer" (`slayerLabel`; a capitalized bracket, the item's own wording, moves after it, "Undead Slayer (Silver)", and a lower-case one that only describes the group goes, "Exorcism (demons)" reads "Exorcism Slayer"), and the chip and token read "Slayer: Dragon Slayer". Matching is unchanged: picking a lesser slayer finds the items with exactly that slayer, never its super slayer, which a muted line at the foot of the popover explains. A shard without `slayerGroups` gets the plain A–Z list.
- **No pager.** The table is virtual: the body holds only the rows in view between two spacer rows, patched rather than replaced so a focused row is never detached, and rows load from `GET /api/items` in 500-row chunks as they scroll into view. A new query keeps the old rows on screen until its first chunk lands; the header (sort arrow, grouped columns) follows the data, not the click.
- **Keyboard.** The toolbar is one tab stop (`role=toolbar`, ←/→ between controls, the search's arrows move its caret until an end); the table is one tab stop (↑/↓, Home/End, Page Up/Down), row actions are reached with Tab from the focused row, and `/` focuses the search.
- **Bridge gating.** Row actions are always drawn and disabled with a reason (`bridge.mts`'s `bridgeActionReason`: worn, client can't, no position for Go to, bridge offline); `pollBridge` dispatches `bridgechange` on `document` when the bridge's state changes, and the table redraws its rows.
- **Columns.** Name, Rarity, Slot and Location are always shown (Slot is left out of a browser fixed to one slot, the Suit Builder's picker, where it would read the same on every row); every other column is the player's choice. The item's tags (Cursed, Prized, …) are their own Tags column, drawn right after Name wherever the list names it and on by default; the Name cell holds only the name and a stack's quantity.
- **Preferences.** The chosen columns (`cols`), the column set they were saved against (`colsVersion`: a list saved before a column joined the defaults gets it once, then an "off" sticks — `view-state.mts`'s `colsFromPrefs`) the row density (`density`: `dense` 32 px or `regular` 40 px) and the column widths dragged on the header edges or set with ←/→ on a focused resize handle (`colWidths`: `{colKey: px}`, 40 to 1200, cleared by table settings' "Reset column widths") are ui-prefs fields. Every screen writes its ui-prefs fields through `ui/prefs.mts`'s `prefs.set(patch)`, the one `PUT /api/ui-prefs` caller, whose failure toast is `messages.mts`'s `prefsSaveFailed` (a `quiet` write, such as the sidebar's, says nothing).
- **Item peek** (`ui/peek.mts`): a row click, Enter or Space opens the selected item in a panel docked beside the table (400 px, 360 below 1280, laid over the table below 1180); while it is open, ↑/↓ step through rows with the peek following and Esc closes it (focus goes back to the row) from the table, the panel or the bare page — a click on the panel's text leaves focus on `<body>`, so the keys are heard on the document; a field being typed in, a popover, a dialog or a drawer keeps its own keys. Its tag chips carry the shard's meaning for each tag (the rules' `tagInfo`, `dom.mts`'s `tagChip`) as a tooltip on hover and focus. Its footer's Highlight, Grab and Go to are gated like the row actions.
- **Item tooltip** (`dom.mts` `tipNode`, `installTooltip`, `showItemTip`, `itemTip`; issue #10): the one `#tip`, always dark, 280 px, shown 400 ms after the pointer settles on anything with `data-serial`, or after an item has had keyboard focus for 400 ms (with `aria-describedby="tip"` on it while open); `pointer-events: none`, placed inside the window. Wherever the page draws an item it wires it with `itemTip(node, item, {focus})`, which sets `data-serial`, registers the record drawn and, unless `focus` is false, makes the node focusable and shows the tooltip on keyboard focus. `ui/item-tip.mts` resolves what to show: the registered record when it carries its tooltip lines, else `state.itemCache`, else one `GET /api/items/by-serial` (a serial the server says it does not know is asked for once per inventory, and a partial record, if registered, is shown instead; a lookup that failed on the network is asked again on the next hover). Wired today: Inventory rows (list, and grouped rows of a single stack: a group of several stacks has no one item to show), the Suit Builder's current suit, Plan, Fetch list and compare cells, character sheet slots, the House map's contents drawer rows and bags, Organize's plan moves and failed steps, and the Scrolls view's detail rows. It hides on leave, Esc, a scroll that moves its item from under the pointer (not the scroll a hover makes to bring a row into view; a keyboard tooltip follows its item instead) and when its item leaves the page; a `data-no-tip` region inside an item (row actions) counts as off it; an item whose own pop or menu is open (`aria-expanded="true"`, the character sheet's `.item-pop`) shows none; inside an open modal dialog (the top layer) `#tip` moves into the dialog so it draws above it, and back to `<body>` when it hides (`dom.mts` holds the element, so a dialog's removal never takes it); the Missing items dialog's rows are wired, with the record the dialog drew since the server no longer has those items. `scripts/ui-tooltips.test.mts` hovers an item on every screen, so a new screen that draws items without `itemTip` fails there.
- **Item names** (issue #181): every screen shows `item.name`, which the fold sets from `vault-lib.mts`'s `displayName`. It is the in-game name, except a Scroll of Transcendence, named by the skill and points on its tooltip ("Scroll of Transcendence (Animal Lore - 0.1 Pts)"), so its rows, groups and name sort tell the skills apart; the in-game name stays the tooltip's first line (`lines[0]`, read by `gameName`), and the item tooltip (`tipNode`) shows it muted under the title as "In game: <name>" whenever it differs from the shown name. Classify this… names the in-game name in its "every item named" scope, and the fold looks up a name override by the shown name, then the in-game name, then the graphic. Organize sends the bridge the in-game name for an item whose shown name is longer than a trip allows. The search also matches the name with its punctuation read as spaces ("transcendence animal lore"), and every name sort (the Inventory's, the Containers view's missing list, the House map's pins and callouts) goes through `vault-lib.mts`'s `compareNames`, which compares numbers by value.
- **Responsive.** Below 1180 px the unset facet chips fold into "+ Filter" (the Weapon skill chip already below 1280 px, where the toolbar has no room for it) and List | Grouped moves into the table settings popover, so the toolbar never wraps.
- **Classify this…** (`ui/kinds.mts`, issue #150) in a row's ⋯ menu, for an item that is neither gear nor a scanned container: a dialog with the kind (every kind but gear and container, which Organize never moves), whether it applies to every item with this name (the default) or with this graphic, and **Reset to automatic** when one of the player's kinds applies (it takes away the entry the dialog names: the name's when there is one, else the graphic's). It saves through `POST /api/item-kinds` and runs `reload()`, so the table, the Kind filter and the Organize plan follow at once. Organize's catch-all row and the ⋯ menu of Auto organize's Other rule have **Review unclassified items**, the Inventory filtered to kind `other` (`showKind`). Settings › Data has **Item kinds you set** with Export… (a download of `item-kinds.json`: a Save dialog in Electron, the browser's download elsewhere) and Import… (a file input; `POST /api/item-kinds/import` merges it in and the toast says what it left out).
- **Containers view** (`ui/containers.mts`): one row per root container, grouped by character with the ground last, with a Fill column and a ⋯ menu (Show these items, Label…, Blacklist…, Forget…). A root with items missing since its last scan (issue #99) shows an "N missing" warn tag after its name and gets **Show missing items**: a dialog listing each item's name, amount (a stack that shrank in place reads "N fewer") and when it was last seen there, sorted by name, fetched from `GET /api/missing?root=`; `GET /api/inventory` carries only the counts (`missingCounts`). The server computes them beside the fold in `app/missing.mts`: a root's previous scan against its latest, less anything the fold still has somewhere else (moved to another scanned container, or worn), and less what a vanished stack could have been merged into: the whole amount of each same graphic, hue and name stack seen elsewhere since the previous scan, and what one in the root grew by (Organize drops items onto matching stacks, and only the receiving stack's serial lives on). Only the shortfall is reported, as "N fewer", so a sale can be under-reported while more of that kind is kept elsewhere; a merge is never reported.
- **Scrolls view** (`ui/scrolls.mts`, issue #181): the Inventory's third view, power scrolls and Scrolls of Transcendence per skill against the shard's Scroll Binder recipes (the rules' `scrollBinder`, `docs/shard-rules.md`); every number comes from `ui/scrolls-model.mts` (no DOM, tested by `app/scrolls-model.test.mts`). The scrolls are three `GET /api/items` queries (power scrolls by `prop=psLevel:1`, Scrolls of Transcendence by `prop=sotPoints:0.1`, `q=scroll binder`), fetched when the view shows and again after a reload; the page holds no item list it could use instead. A refetch that fails while earlier scrolls are shown says so above the table, so they read as out of date. A toolbar with a skill filter ("/" focuses it) and Power scrolls | Scrolls of Transcendence with their counts, a line of facts ("95 power scrolls across 31 skills · 28 Scrolls of Transcendence across 15 skills · 1 empty Scroll Binder · Nothing is ready to bind yet"; a skill ready in both tabs counts once), then one table per tab in the Items view's card. The facts line is a live region, redrawn only when its words change, so typing in the filter does not announce it again. **Power scrolls:** Skill, a count per level (105 to 120, blank for none; each count links to the Items view searching for exactly those scrolls, `powerQuery`: "scroll of provocation (110 skill)", which in Grouped mode is their one grouped row), Next roll-up (the step needing the fewest more, the higher tier on a tie: "110 → 115: 5 of 12", a meter labeled in words, "7 more") and, only when some skill can bind now and the detail is closed, Bind everything (what binding every full set would leave, each binding counting toward the level above before it binds: "1 × 115, 4 × 110"; blank on a row that binds nothing). Rows sort by fewest more needed, then the higher tier, then name; a row that can bind now has a Ready badge and a green edge. **Scrolls of Transcendence:** Skill, the scrolls' points as chips (equal points share one chip, "18 × 0.3", and a long row wraps in its cell; hidden while the detail is open), Total, Progress (a meter against the usable totals, 2.0 marked and 5.0 at its end, its value held at 5.0 past it and `aria-valuetext` the real total, "5.4 points") and Binder plan: the fewest scrolls adding up to exactly 5.0 when they can, else exactly 2.0 ("Bind 1.0 + 0.6 + 0.4 → 2.0"); with neither and 5.0 or more in all, the set that goes past 5.0 least, then with the fewest scrolls, saying what is lost ("Bind 17 × 0.3 → 5.0 (0.1 lost)"; equal scrolls are written as a count); else how far short of the next usable total ("0.4 short of 2.0"); points are whole tenths, never floats. A warning above the table spells out the binder's trap (past 2.0 it locks until 5.0; above 5.0 is lost). The footers give the recipe and the order. Sorted columns carry `aria-sort` and an arrow; the order is fixed, so the headers are not buttons. A shard without `scrollBinder` gets the plain holdings: Skill, the level counts, Total and Where (the places by name) for power scrolls sorted by skill, and Skill, Scrolls, Total and Where for Transcendence, with no roll-up, plan, warning or binder facts. With no scrolls at all the table says "No power scrolls or Scrolls of Transcendence in your scans." **Empty Scroll Binders** are counted in the facts: a binder whose tooltip has nothing past its name but its weight and state lines (Blessed, Insured, a tag). A partly filled binder is ignored, in that count and in the roll-ups, until a scan shows how its tooltip says what it holds. Stat scrolls are not shown: no scan has shown how the shard names them. **Detail:** a row click, Enter or Space opens a panel in the item peek's look (`#scr-peek`, laid over the table below 1180 px): the skill, ↑ / ↓ / close, how many scrolls in how many containers, the roll-up (Next roll-up, Still needed, Bind everything when something binds, a meter) or the binder plan, then the scrolls grouped by place (the place's name and serial, who scanned it: "On the ground · scanned by Kestrel"), each row a level or points badge, the name and its tags, wired with `itemTip`; its footer has **Show in Inventory** (the Items view searching for all that skill's scrolls). ↑/↓ on a row move focus and, while the panel is open, the panel follows; Esc closes it and gives the row its focus back.
