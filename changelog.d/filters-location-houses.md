- **Filter by house**: the Location filter now lists your houses from the House map, each with its item count, so you can show only one house, or a few together, alongside backpacks and banks. Each house's containers are listed under its name. Picking a house takes every container in it, including ones a later scan adds.
- For contributors: `ItemQuery.houses` (`house=<id>`, repeated), matched through `applyItemQuery`'s `houseOf` (container serial → house id), which `GET /api/items` and Move to's `resolveSelection` build with `HousesService.houseOfContainers` and the page with `house-links.mts`'s `houseIds`. `claimedHouses` in `app/store/house-claims.mts` is the one claim filter for the house routes and the item query.
