- For contributors: **the doll card's Customize popover** (issue #57): race, gender, skin, hair and facial hair from the game's own character-creation lists (`app/paperdoll-creation.mts`, ClassicUO's `CharacterCreationValues`), so only valid combinations are offered. It is session-only (per character, dropped by a reload or a newer scan, `app/ui/customize-model.mts`), with a "Customized" tag and Reset. `GET /api/paperdoll/options` sends the choices with swatch colors from hues.mul and names from the client's Cliloc.enu (`app/cliloc.mts`, BWT-packed on 7.x). The palettes are the client's picker entries, and the hue a character gets is one more, which the live scan confirms.
