- **Scans record your character's look** (issue #57): the TazUO scanner and Character refresh now save your character's body, skin, hair, beard and face, ready for the paperdoll preview coming in a later release. Reinstall the scripts from Settings to pick it up; the app keeps the look from your newest scan that has one.
- For contributors: **an `appearance` block in the scan format** (issue #57): an optional top-level `appearance: {body, hue, hair, beard, face}` (each layer `{graphic, hue}` or `null`) in `app/schema/scan.v2.schema.json` and `docs/scan-schema.md`, written by TazUO 2.22.0 through the shared `adapters/_shared/tazuo/appearance.py`. The fold keeps a character's last-known look on `Character.appearance` when a newer scan has none, and retention keeps the scan that supplied it.
