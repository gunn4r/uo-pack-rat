- **For contributors: the TazUO panel's prefs have a store** (issue #338): `app/store/panel-prefs.mts` reads and writes `tazuo-panel.json` (`readPanelPrefs` and `writePanelPrefs` moved there from `app/tazuo-panel.mts`), and its `save(change)` merges a change over the file as it is on disk through `update`. `ServerContext` carries `panelPrefsStore` in place of the raw `panelPrefsFile` path and the `savePanel` closure. The file's bytes are the same.
