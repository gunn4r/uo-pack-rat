- For contributors: **the paperdoll renderer** (issue #57): `app/paperdoll.mts` composes a character's figure from the client's own gumps the way ClassicUO's paperdoll does. That covers the body gump, each piece's gump from its tiledata anim through `Equipconv.def` and `gump.def`, `PaperdollOrder`'s draw order and `IsCovered`'s hidden layers, checked against ClassicUO's own code on 200,000 random worn sets. Two rules are its own: a `0x0001` lining pixel is drawn only over something, and a body outside the human, elf and gargoyle set gets the stand-in. Also new: the crop box for a pair of figures and the stand-in's grey. Nothing calls it yet; the image route comes next.
