- **For contributors: `house-claims.json` has a schema, and its cases are shared** (issue #338): `app/schema/house-claims.v1.schema.json` describes one claimed house, the app checks entries against it, and `app/fixtures/contract-cases/house-claims.json` holds accept and reject cases that both the app's reader and the TazUO scripts' reader run, so the two can't drift apart. The cases found one drift already: the TazUO scripts (2.21.0) now drop a claim whose id ends in a newline or uses digits that aren't ASCII, as the app always did.
