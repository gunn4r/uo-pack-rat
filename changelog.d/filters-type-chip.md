- **A Type filter**: a new Type chip beside Kind narrows the Inventory and the Suit Builder's Manual picker to Shield, Two-handed weapon, One-handed weapon, Spellbook, Armor, Jewelry, Clothing or Other, each with its count. In the Weapon 2H / Shield picker it offers just Two-handed weapon and Shield, so "only shields" is two clicks. Picking a type with no weapons hides the Weapon skill chip. Organize rules and the MCP `search_items` tool take types too.
- For contributors: `itemTypeOf`, `ITEM_TYPES`, `ITEM_TYPE_LABELS` and `typesOfSlot` in `app/domain/pools.mts`; the `type` query param (any of), the facets' `types`, an optional `type` on a rule's query (checked by `checkOrganizeConfig`), and `types` on MCP's `inventory_facets` and `search_items`.
