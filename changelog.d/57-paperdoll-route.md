- For contributors: **`GET /api/paperdoll.png`** (issue #57): a character's figure as a PNG, drawn by `app/paperdoll.mts` from the UO folder's gumps, tiledata and hues (with `gump.def` and `Equipconv.def`). The page sends the look and the worn pieces as `graphic:hue` pairs; the server takes each piece's layer from tiledata. `pair` names the other figure of a side-by-side pair, so both sides come out the same size, and `gray=1` draws the stand-in gray. It answers like item art: an ETag and a 304, and a 404 with a reason word, never a path. The figures share item art's 4 MB PNG cache, and a renderer over the open files is kept while their stamps stay the same.
