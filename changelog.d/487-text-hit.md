- **Find a character by skill**: the Characters search now also matches a character's top skills ("magery"), as the Suit Builder's character picker already did. Every list search in the app (option lists, Runs, the roster, the Move to picker, the buff picker) now follows one rule: the typed text, in any case, anywhere in the row.
- For contributors: `textHit(needle, ...fields)` in `app/ui/format.mts` is that rule (#487); the option lists, `runs.mts`, `roster.mts`'s `rosterView`, `move-model.mts`'s `matchesDest`, `scrolls-model.mts`'s `filterRows`, the combobox's `filterGroups` and the buff picker read it, and `RosterRow.skills` carries the top skills.
