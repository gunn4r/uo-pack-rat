- **For contributors: the page's shared modules are listed once** (issue #338): `app/http/routes/static.mts` serves `/<path>.mjs` for every `app/<path>.*` entry of `tsconfig.browser.json`'s `include`, read once at start, through one route instead of 17 hand-written ones. To share a module with the page, add it to the include; the copies of the list in `scripts/build-ui.mts` and the docs now point there. The tsconfig ships with the app for this. The same 17 URLs are served.
