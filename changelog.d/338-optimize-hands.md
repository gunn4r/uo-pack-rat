- **The Suit Builder never starts from a two-handed weapon beside a one-hander** (issue #338): the game never wears both, but a hand-built `POST /api/optimize` could send such a worn suit (and the demo character Dorran wears a spellbook beside a two-handed staff). The quick search emptied the one hand while the exact search kept it, so the two could disagree. Now the pools leave the one hand empty under a worn two-handed weapon (the piece stays a candidate), so the exact search reads such a worn suit the way the quick search always did, and a hand-built request wearing both is refused with a 400 "current: a two-handed weapon leaves the one-hand slot empty", as Manual's Fill already is. `docs/solver.md` lists the hand rule among the rules written twice.
