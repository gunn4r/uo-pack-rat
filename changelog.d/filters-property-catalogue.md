- **A clean, complete property list in the Property rule**: it lists every gear property, by the shard's names with their units ("Hit Chance Increase %", "Weapon Speed s"), in clear groups (Resists, Casting, Combat, Weapon, Defense & eaters, Stats & pools, Other, Yes or no). Lines from Bulk Order Deeds, tools and books no longer show up as properties. Weapon Speed, the damage types, Range, the eaters, Hit Mana Drain, Hit Fatigue, Splintering Weapon, Velocity, Soul Charge, Lower Requirements and more now read as proper names in chips and columns too.
- **Counts in the Property rule**: each property says how many items in view carry it ("Faster Casting 46"), in the Inventory and in the Suit Builder's Manual picker. A property nothing in view has stays listed, dimmed at 0, so you can see you don't have it.
- **Every slayer in the Slayer filter**: all of the shard's slayers are listed, the ones you have none of dimmed at 0. Power scroll level and Scroll of Transcendence points leave the Property rule's list; a saved rule or link that uses them still works.
- For contributors: `PROPERTY_CATALOGUE` in `app/domain/properties.mts` is the property rule's list (registry keys, and the `extras` key for a tooltip line no property models, so saved rules keep matching); `labelOf`/`fullOf` read its names. `GET /api/items?…&fields=props` answers `propCounts` (`ItemPropCountsApiResponse`), which `ItemSource.propCounts` fetches. `FLAG_CATALOGUE` is its Yes or no group, `extraKeys` is gear-only, and `slayerTree` lists every slayer of the rules' table.
