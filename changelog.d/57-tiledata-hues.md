- For contributors: **tiledata and hues for the paperdoll** (issue #57): `readTileData`'s `TileInfo` now carries each item's `layer` and `anim` (which names its paperdoll gump), and `FLAG.partialHue` names the partial-hue flag. `hueFor` in `app/art.mts` gives a hue's colours with the partial switch the caller passes: always for the body's skin, the tiledata flag for worn items, hair, beard and face. `hueOf` now treats a hue number equal to the file's count as no hue, as ClassicUO does. `scripts/gen-graphic-layers.mts` reads layers through `readTileData` instead of its own byte offsets.
