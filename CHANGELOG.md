# Changelog

## Unreleased

- **For contributors: a module map and change checklists** (issue #218): `docs/module-map.md` says where each module and concept lives, `docs/change-checklists.md` lists everything else to change for each kind of change, the pull request template asks which kinds a change is, and `CLAUDE.md` is a short guide for coding agents. A test keeps the map current.
- **Fix: Razor Enhanced scans classify worn items by their layer** (issue #219): Razor names nine paperdoll layers differently from TazUO (`RightHand`, `LeftHand`, `Head`, `Neck`, `InnerTorso`, `OuterLegs` and the rest), and the app ignored those names and guessed a worn piece's slot from its graphic or name. It now reads them as the same layers, so a worn shield, weapon, chest piece, kilt or gorget lands in its slot. Your scans are unchanged.
- **Ask Claude Code about your gear** (issue #211): Pack Rat has a built-in MCP server, off by default. Turn it on in **Settings › AI assistants (MCP)**, copy the one-line `claude mcp add …` command (or the JSON for another MCP client), and a Claude Code session can search your inventory with the Inventory's filters, read an item, a container, a character sheet, your scrolls and saved runs, compare runs, check how fresh your scans are, run the Suit Builder with a profile, buffs and pinned pieces, score a hand-picked suit, and preview Auto organize. A second switch, **Allow in-game actions**, also lets it Highlight, Go to, Grab and run Organize trips through the same bridge and checks as the buttons. The address and token stay the same across restarts; **New token** replaces the token. What the client reads, it may send to its own AI provider. `docs/mcp.md` has the details.
- **The Suit Builder's Automatic search uses every gear slot** (issue #202): it now picks boots, a shirt, a doublet or sash, a robe, a belt or apron, earrings and a kilt when they help, beside the twelve slots it filled before. Each of these may stay empty (a plain piece you wear, such as boots with no properties, stays on rather than being planned off), and the one-hand/two-hand rule is unchanged. The result's Plan and Fetch list, compare and the Locked slots chip list them in Manual's order, and Manual's **Fill the rest automatically** searches them too. Runs saved before still open, compare and open in Manual with the slots they planned, but a new build no longer reuses them.
- **Fix: gold and other metal necklaces no longer count as blocking meditation** (issue #202): a Gold Necklace or Gold Beads was read as gold armor, so the Inventory's meditation filter hid it and the Suit Builder left it out of a meditation-safe suit. In the neck slot only a gorget, a mempo or a collar is armor now.
- **Kilts and skirts get their own slot** (issue #202): a cloth kilt, skirt, hakama or fur sarong is worn over pants or armor legs, so it now has its own **Kilt / Skirt** slot instead of sharing Legs, and Manual shows it beside Legs (nineteen slots). Such pieces no longer count as legs: the Automatic search leaves them out of the legs slot for now, and Organize files them with Other gear instead of Armor (Simple) or Armor: legs (Detailed). A worn kilt shows on the character sheet. Items with no known graphic are now named into the right slot more often, such as "Platemail Legs", shirts, a formal shirt, a full apron, gargish kilts (hands), wing armor, gargish glasses (earrings), talons, bracers, dresses, kimonos, collars, amulets, mitts and a Flower Garland, and bare beads are no longer a necklace; an item whose graphic is known was already in its right slot. Your scans are unchanged, and a Manual suit or a saved run holding a piece that moved shows it in its new slot.
- **Hand suits back and forth between Manual and Automatic** (issue #12): Manual's suit card has **Fill the rest automatically**, which searches only the empty slots with the Automatic panel's requirements, weights, pool settings and resist caps and Manual's buffs, keeping every piece you placed. A placed sash is searched like any piece, so its Lower Reagent Cost lowers the LRC the search looks for. It shows its progress with Cancel, fills the slots in one undo step and says how it went ("Filled 5 slots · proven optimal", and which requirements the suit still misses); it works with No character too, from the pieces nobody wears. **Save as run** keeps a manual suit in the Saved runs drawer, marked Manual, and a result's **Start from this result** or a run's **Open in Manual** loads it into Manual in one undo step, with the buffs it was planned with. Manual also gets the result's **Fetch list** with Go to and Grab, and its Saved runs button back.
- **Plan an automatic suit around your buffs** (issue #12): the Suit Builder's Automatic panel has a **Buffs** section above Requirements. **Add buff** opens the same picker as Manual's, and the buffs you turn on count as always on: with Divine Fury's SSI +10, a 60 SSI requirement needs only 50 from gear, so the search spends the rest on something else; with its DCI −20, gear has to make up the loss. Each requirement a buff changes says what gear still needs ("Gear needs 50: Divine Fury gives 10"). Cap changes count too (White Tiger's DCI cap, Stone Form's resist caps, Magic Reflection's Physical cap, Corpse Skin and Curse). A bonus past the cap, such as Enemy of One's damage, never changes the plan. If you raised a resist cap for a form (Fire 95 for Reaper Form) and also turn the form on, the plan uses the shard's cap so the loss isn't counted twice, and says so. The result reads **Planned with** and the buffs, and **Show without buffs** shows the same suit's totals and requirements without them. Potions and Enchant are worked out from what the character wears now. The buffs are remembered per character, saved with each run (its name says "+Divine Fury" when they changed), and **Load these settings** brings back the buffs and the numbers they used.
- **Fix: the Suit Builder no longer plans STR, DEX or INT from gear past 150** (issue #12): gear stat bonuses take a stat up to 150 on UO Alive, so a character at raw DEX 125 gets at most 25 DEX from gear (5 with a Greater Agility potion's +20), and the search spends the rest elsewhere. Runs saved before this are not reused for a character.
- **Count buffs, forms and abilities in a manual suit** (issue #12): Manual's totals card has a **Buffs** row. **Add buff** opens a picker beside the slots with Divine Fury, Consecrate Weapon and Enemy of One, the Necromancy forms, Reaper Form, Bless and its single-stat spells, Protection, Reactive Armor, Magic Reflection, Stone Form, Enchant, Animal Form (Wolf or Bake Kitsune, Ki-Rin), White Tiger Form, Honorable Execution, the bard songs and other masteries, Strength and Agility potions, the Eodon potions, Grapes of Wrath, fish pies and skill tinctures, Arcane Empowerment, Attunement, Ethereal Form, the smaller Animal Forms, Mysterious Wisp, Rampage, Playing the Odds, Curse Weapon, the Human's +2 HPR and the Gargoyle's passives and Berserk, and the debuffs an enemy can cast on you (Curse, Corpse Skin, Mind Rot) in their own folded group, each with its numbers worked out from your character's skills (change a skill to plan a level you don't have yet). Where the shard wiki and the server code disagree, the wiki's number counts and the other is noted; numbers not yet checked in game are marked unverified, and items that may not exist on UO Alive say so. One form at a time: turning on another says which one it replaced, with Undo. The totals, the stats and every piece's Change column then count the buffs: a total they move shows by how much, a cap they raise (White Tiger's DCI, Stone Form's resists) is underlined, and a bonus past the cap (Enemy of One, Consecrate Weapon) is shown apart from the capped total. Bless and the potions share each stat, the largest counting. Turn **Count buffs in the totals** off to see the suit without them; the buffs and the switch are remembered, and turning a buff on or off can be undone.
- **Build a suit by hand in the Suit Builder** (issue #12): a new **Automatic | Manual** switch beside the Suit Builder's title. **Manual** shows every gear slot as a compact card (the twelve the optimizer fills plus feet, shirt, tunic, robe, waist and earrings), a totals strip above them (the five resists, LRC, LMC, FC, FCR, SSI, DCI, HCI and DI against their caps, a total past its cap shown at the cap with "+N wasted", and STR, DEX and INT), and a picker. Click a slot and the picker lists every piece that fits it, with all the Inventory's filters, and a **Change** column saying what each piece would do to the totals ("LRC +20 → 77 · LMC +8 → 35"); a piece someone wears or a gargoyle-only piece is tagged. Click a piece (or press Enter) to put it in the slot; the picker stays on that slot so you can try others, and Esc closes it. A two-handed weapon and a one-handed weapon never share the hands: picking one clears the other and says so. The character picker decides the totals: a character's Resisting Spells and race caps, or **No character** for raw item totals. **Start from what <name> wears** fills the slots from that character's suit, each card has Clear, and **Clear all** empties the suit. Under the suit, the character sheet's stats and properties show what the character would have in this suit. **⌘Z** / **⇧⌘Z** (Ctrl+Z / Ctrl+Y on Windows and Linux) or the Undo and Redo buttons step back and forward through the last 40 changes to the suit. The mode and the suit are remembered across a restart; a piece no longer in your scans shows as missing.
- **Put away, Organize trips and Grab close the containers they opened — re-install the scripts (TazUO 2.15.0)** (issue #196): after a **Put away**, every bag and chest the trip opened stayed open on screen. The bridge now closes the container windows a trip or a Grab opened once it ends, whether it finished, failed or was stopped, innermost first. A container you already had open stays open, and so do your backpack and bank box. A Highlight still leaves its containers open, to show you where the item is.
- **Fix: the Suit Builder's weapon exclusions now hold for Use Best Weapon Skill weapons without a Skill Required line** (issue #193): on UO Alive such a weapon (a Sledge Hammer, for one) shows no Skill Required line, so excluding every melee skill still let it into the suit. It swings with a melee skill either way, so it now stays out unless a melee skill is allowed and the Use Best Weapon Skill switch is on.
- **The Slayer filter is laid out like the shard's slayer charts** (issue #189): instead of one A–Z list, the Inventory's Slayer filter lists each super slayer with a small **Super** tag, such as **Reptile Slayer**, with its lesser slayers indented under it (**Dragon Slayer**, **Snake Slayer**), then the talisman slayers and, under **Other**, anything else. Only the slayers you own are listed; a super slayer you own none of still heads its lesser slayers. Picking a slayer still finds exactly the items with that slayer: a super slayer also hits its lesser slayers' creatures, but at double damage instead of triple, as a note in the filter says. The grouping is part of the shard rules (`slayerGroups`, docs/shard-rules.md).
- **Filter by weapon skill** (issue #188): the Inventory has a **Weapon skill** filter next to Slot and Kind, listing Archery, Swordsmanship, Fencing, Mace Fighting and Throwing with how many weapons each has. Pick one or more to see only those weapons, for example only your swords. A weapon with **Use Best Weapon Skill** counts under Swordsmanship, Fencing and Mace Fighting, since it swings with whichever of the three is highest. **Save as rule…** carries the filter into an Organize rule.
- **Use Best Weapon Skill weapons in the Suit Builder** (issue #187): excluding weapon skills used to drop a weapon with Use Best Weapon Skill whenever its own skill was excluded, so "Swordsmanship only" left out a kryss that swings with your Swordsmanship. Such a weapon swings with your best of Swordsmanship, Fencing and Mace Fighting, so it now enters the pool while any of those three is allowed, whatever its own skill. The Weapons popover has a new check, **Allow any weapon with Use Best Weapon Skill**, on by default (profiles, templates and runs saved before keep it on); clear it to hold those weapons to their own skill. The chip says so when it matters: "Weapons: Swordsmanship only, plus Use Best Weapon Skill".
- **Filter by yes/no properties** (issue #182): the Inventory's **Property rule…** now also lists the properties an item either has or not, such as **Spell Channeling**, **Mage Armor** and **Night Sight**, in a **Yes or no** group at the end. Pick one and there is no comparison or number to fill in: **Add rule** keeps only the items that have it, and the filter shows as a token you can remove. Several of them must all be present. **Save as rule…** carries them into an Organize rule. Slayers keep their own Slayer filter, and tags their Tags filter.
- **Inventory › Scrolls: your power scrolls and Scrolls of Transcendence, rolled up for the Scroll Binder** (issue #181): the Inventory's view switch has a third option, **Scrolls** (`#/scrolls`). The Power scrolls tab lists each skill once with how many scrolls you hold at 105, 110, 115 and 120 (each count opens the Items view on exactly those scrolls), the next roll-up the binder could make ("110 → 115: 5 of 12", "7 more"), sorted by how few more you need, and, for skills that can bind now, what binding everything would leave you with. The Scrolls of Transcendence tab (`#/scrolls/sot`) adds each skill's points up and plans a binder that lands on exactly 2.0 or 5.0 with the fewest scrolls ("Bind 1.0 + 0.6 + 0.4 → 2.0"), or, when no set lands exactly and you have 5.0 or more, the one that goes past 5.0 least, saying what is lost; otherwise it says how far short you are, with a warning that going past 2.0 locks the binder until 5.0. Click a skill for its scrolls by container, with Show in Inventory. The header counts your empty Scroll Binders; partly filled binders are left out until a scan shows how their tooltips read. The recipes are part of the shard rules (`scrollBinder`, docs/shard-rules.md); a shard without them lists what you hold per skill and where.
- **Scrolls of Transcendence say their skill** (issue #181): instead of dozens of identical "Scroll Of Transcendence" rows, each scroll is named by the skill and points on its tooltip, such as **Scroll of Transcendence (Animal Lore - 0.1 Pts)**, everywhere the app shows an item: Inventory rows and groups, the peek, the item tooltip, Containers, the House map, Organize, the Suit Builder and the character sheet. Grouped, scrolls of the same skill and points share a row; sorted by name, they go by skill, then points. Search for "transcendence animal lore" or "animal lore" to find them, and filter on the new **SoT pts** property. The item tooltip shows the name the game uses under it ("In game: Scroll Of Transcendence"), and a kind you set for "Scroll Of Transcendence" still applies. Your scans are unchanged.

Long runs of empty tiles are squeezed into narrow strips, so the cards sit close together. Your areas show as tinted outlines behind their cards, with a name pill that shows only that area when clicked. Click a card to select its stack, or a container to open it in the contents drawer; the arrow keys move between cards and Enter selects. - **Organize follows a bag a trip moved** (issue #153): after a trip moved a bag (gathering an empty bag, say), Organize still placed the bag, and everything in it, in the chest it came out of until the next scan. A later trip that put items into that bag sent the bridge to the old chest, and the bridge refused every put ("is not inside ... rescan and try again"). - **Classify this…: give an item the kind Pack Rat missed** (issue #150): anything the kind patterns did not recognise fell into Other, and there was no way to fix it. **Classify this…** in an item's ⋯ menu in Inventory now gives it a kind for every item with its name (the default) or every item with its graphic, and **Reset to automatic** takes that back; the inventory and the Organize plan change at once, with no rescan. **Review unclassified items**, beside the container for everything else on the Organize screen and in the menu of Auto organize's Other rule, opens the Inventory filtered to kind Other so you can sweep it. Your kinds live in `item-kinds.json` in the data folder, over Pack Rat's own list rather than a copy of it, so later improvements still reach you; a name beats a graphic, and gear always keeps its own kind. **Settings › Data › Item kinds you set** exports them to a file and imports one, merging it into yours. A hand-edited file that does not parse is set aside as `item-kinds.json.corrupt`, and an entry with a kind Pack Rat does not know is left out with a warning in the log.
- **Decor, quest and event items, and crafting tools are kinds of their own** (issue #150, part 1): items the kind patterns missed fell into Other, where Organize could only file them as one lump. The Inventory's Kind filter and the rule editor now offer three more kinds: **decor** (statuettes, an ethereal mount's statuette included, statues, paintings, trophies, rugs, potted plants, vases, banners, tapestries, lamps, candles, ornaments and anything named Decorative …), **quest** (a Mysterious Fragment, anything named Quest, keepsakes and the Halloween, Christmas, Easter, Valentine's and holiday items; event tokens stay currency, holiday deeds stay deeds, an event bag or gift box stays a container) and **crafting** (the tools a crafting menu opens from: smith's hammer, tongs, sewing kit, tinker's tools and tool kit, mortar and pestle, fletcher's tools, the carpentry saws, planes and knives, mapmaker's and scribe's pens, rolling pin, flour sifter, skillet, loom, spinning wheel). Pickaxes, shovels, lockpicks, fishing poles, skinning knives, scissors and the instruments stay tools, and so does a conjured Healing Stone, which used to be Other. Springs and a Clock Frame are resources, and a book named Guide is a book. Auto organize files the new kinds: Simple and By build add a Decor and a Quest & event items group, and Tools takes the crafting tools by a second rule; Detailed adds a Crafting tools group too. When there are fewer chests than groups, Decor and Quest & event items share the Other chest (what they hold used to be Other), so they never take a chest another group needs. A group that comes to share a chest takes a bag there and leaves the group already filling the chest where it is, so re-running Auto organize on a sorted house moves only the items of the new groups. An Auto setup saved before this release keeps filing crafting tools with its Tools rule until Auto organize runs again (the rule editor opens that rule with the crafting kind ticked, so saving it keeps them); a rule of your own that asks for the tool kind takes tools only, so add the crafting kind to it if it should take crafting tools too.
- **A Grab frees its chest's slot for Organize and Put away** (issue #148): Inventory's Grab moved an item into your backpack, but Organize still counted the chest it came from as it was last scanned, so a grab from a full chest left no room to put the item back: Put away backpack said "1 with no room" for it. - **Auto organize gives a group's surplus chests to groups that need them** (issue #146): a group used to keep every chest an earlier setup gave it, or that held only its items, even when it needed fewer, so after a Detailed run proposing Simple could keep Jewelry in three chests while Skill scrolls and Spell scrolls squeezed into one, with items left without room and the drawer still saying "This is already your setup". Where keeping those chests leaves items with no room, a group now keeps them only as far as it needs them and the rest go to the groups short of room. A house that is sorted and fits still stays exactly as it is.

### Security

An adversarial review went over the app in seven areas — the local server, the scan and bridge parsers, the script installer, the Electron shell, the adapter scripts, the supply chain, and the privacy claims themselves — and everything below came out of it. `docs/threat-model.md` is new and is the long form: what each boundary is holding, which defence is load-bearing against which attack, and the handful of findings deliberately left standing with the reason for each.

- **Adapter scripts now distrust their own command queue — re-install them.** `<data>/bridge/<client>/queue.jsonl` is an ordinary file that drives your character in a live game, and anything on your machine can write it, so the bridge no longer assumes the app validated a line before acting on it. It refuses a command older than 60 seconds or one it has already run, opens nothing that is not really a container (corpses included) and no chain longer than 8, refuses a walk further than 24 tiles or off the map, and grabs only from your own backpack, your bank, or the container that same command just opened — the destination was always your own backpack and still is. If the queue is written faster than a person clicks, the bridge says so and stops, because at that rate something other than you is writing it. One junk line no longer swallows the rest of a batch, and neither the client nor the status file can be made to grow without bound. **TazUO scripts are now 2.1.0 and Razor Enhanced's 1.1.0: re-install them from the app's Settings tab (Reinstall) or re-run the setup wizard.** These limits have not yet been exercised against a live client — the threat model lists which assumptions would fail first, and a false refusal reports itself in game and on the page.
- **A shared scan file can no longer do anything to the app but be read.** Scans get passed around, so one is treated as a stranger's text end to end: the character sheet and item tooltip are built from DOM nodes with no markup path left anywhere in the page, the schema types and bounds every field instead of leaving whole sections unchecked, the fold and the tooltip parser no longer have shapes whose cost grows with the square of what a file contains, and a file over 32 MB — or one that is a symlink rather than a real file — is refused in the inbox before it is read. A file that fails to parse now says so without quoting its own contents back at you.
- **Every request the local server accepts is checked field by field.** A body that is not a JSON object is refused rather than becoming an internal error; the routes that write files validate what they are about to write; a build request's options are an allowlist with real ranges; at most four builds run at once; the page cannot be put in a frame by another site; requests time out; and the data directory and the files in it are created readable only by you.
- **The installer cannot be redirected out of the folder you picked.** Game clients arrive as unpacked archives, and an archive can contain symlinks. Every file the installer and the scan importer write now goes through one helper that refuses any destination that is not either absent or an ordinary file, and writes through an unpredictable temporary name. A `packrat-paths.json` you wrote by hand, pointing at a different data directory, is now kept instead of silently overwritten.
- **The desktop shell gives less away.** Electron's spellchecker is switched off, so on Windows and Linux the app no longer downloads a dictionary from Google's servers the first time you click into a text box — which `PRIVACY.md` had always said could not happen. The window denies every permission request (camera, microphone, location, clipboard), refuses to navigate anywhere but its own page, and hands only an `https:` link to your normal browser. Packaged builds turn off the Electron fuses that would let the installed program be re-used as a general-purpose script interpreter, keep the app's own code inside the archive rather than as loose files beside it, and ship with developer tools off. "Check for updates" now ignores the link in GitHub's own answer unless it really points at this project's releases.
- **The release path was split so that the job running three hundred build dependencies holds no token.** Building and publishing are separate jobs now: the build matrix has read-only access and no release token at all, and a separate job with no checkout and no dependencies uploads the installers plus a `SHA256SUMS` file you can check a download against (`README.md` and `RELEASING.md` have the command). Every GitHub Action is pinned to an exact commit rather than a movable tag, both workflows install without running package lifecycle scripts, and Dependabot now watches npm and the action pins.
- **The privacy document was corrected where it was wrong.** It overstated two of the server's checks, understated what the data directory holds and what the logs contain, and — the one that mattered — described a scan file as identifying "which character and account it came from" without mentioning that it also carries your full skill and stat profile and the **world coordinates of your character and of every container scanned**, which in practice is your house. `PRIVACY.md` now says all of that plainly, with a warning to look at a scan file before attaching it to a public issue. `SECURITY.md` no longer sends reporters down the public route on the strength of a stale hedge — private vulnerability reporting is on.
- **The bridge only opens containers that lead down from your own root — re-install the scripts (TazUO 2.2.0, Razor Enhanced 1.2.0).** A queue line could name another player's backpack in its container chain, and the bridge would double-click it — a snoop attempt the victim is told about. Each container in a chain is now checked against the live client just before it opens: the first must lie on the ground or be your own backpack or open bank box, and each after it must sit inside the one before. A refused command now shows its reason on the button you clicked, instead of only in game.
