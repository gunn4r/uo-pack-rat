# Code of conduct

This project is small, volunteer-run, and exists so Ultima Online players can build better suits. The expectations here are simple.

## Be civil

Disagree with ideas, not people. Criticize code, not the person who wrote it. Sarcasm, mockery, and pile-ons make people stop contributing — don't do them, and don't join in when someone else does.

## Assume good faith

Most confusing pull requests, blunt issue reports, and awkwardly-worded questions come from someone doing their best, not someone being difficult. Ask a clarifying question before assuming the worst.

## No harassment

Personal attacks, insults, unwelcome contact, or targeting anyone for who they are have no place here. This applies in issues, pull requests, discussions, and any other space this project uses.

## Keep discussion about the software

Issues, pull requests, and discussions are for Pack Rat — bugs, features, code, and how the project runs. Take everything else elsewhere.

## Reporting a problem

If someone's behavior in this project's spaces needs attention, contact the person listed as the repository's owner on GitHub, either by opening a private security advisory (see `SECURITY.md` for how) or by messaging them directly through GitHub. Reports are handled privately; retaliation against someone for reporting in good faith is itself a violation of this policy.

## Consequences

A maintainer may edit, close, or remove content that violates this policy, and may warn, temporarily restrict, or permanently ban anyone whose behavior continues to violate it, at their discretion.
