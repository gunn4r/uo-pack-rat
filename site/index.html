Stop opening chests. Search them.

A free desktop app for Ultima Online players on UO Alive. Degenerate hoarder? Same. Pack Rat finds anything you own in seconds, keeps your house organized without some elaborate system, and builds better suits from gear you already have.

Pack Rat · demo data

Item icons and the paperdoll are drawn from your own Ultima Online client once you install, so they're blank here.

Inventory

Every bag, chest and bank box, in one ledger.

Search everything every character owns. Narrow it by property (at least, at most, exactly or not), type, slot, slayer, house or label. Highlight marks a piece in game, Go to walks you to its chest, Grab puts it in your backpack.

Items

One table of every piece, each with its in-game icon and its name in its rarity's color. Click one for its properties and where it is.

Containers

Every chest and bag you've opened, who scanned it and when, and how full it is out of 125.

Scrolls

Power scrolls by skill and level, with how many you need for the next roll-up, and your Scrolls of Transcendence.

Unravel

The pieces worth unraveling for imbuing ingredients, grouped by what each gives at your Imbuing skill, with a forecast of what you'd get.

Characters

Each character, at a glance.

The paperdoll in the gear they wear, the five resists against the cap, stats, and every property against the shard's cap.

Property caps, rarity colors and the gargoyle race lock follow UO Alive's rules.

Suit Builder

The best suit is already in your house.

Two ways to build it.

Automatic

Pick a template, press Build.

Melee, Caster, Tank or your own goals. The Suit Builder searches everything you own, plans around the buffs you fight with, and proves the suit is the best one. Runs are kept so you can compare.

Manual

Dress the paperdoll yourself.

Pick a piece slot by slot on a live paperdoll and see every number move as you hover. Fill the rest automatically finishes the suit around the pieces you chose.

House map

Your house, drawn from the game. Every chest in it.

Every chest stack colored by its label or by how full it is, at the game's angle, top-down or as a stack plan. Search it and the chests that hold your katana light up.

Organize

Give every item a home.

Three steps, and nothing moves until you say so.

  1. Rules say where things go.

    Armor to the armor chest, reagents to the reagent chest. Each item goes to the first rule it matches.

  2. Or let Auto organize write them.

    Simple, detailed or by build. It labels your chests and shows the plan first.

  3. Then Put away.

    With TazUO the trip runs in game while you watch.
In your own client

A panel in the game, a bridge to the app.

Small scripts in your client. The scanner reads what your character can see; the bridge carries out what you press in the app. They never fight, gather or loot.

Setting up

Five minutes, then scan.

Everything stays in one folder on your computer. The only thing Pack Rat sends anywhere is the update check.

The setup window

Choose UO Alive and your game client. Pack Rat installs its scripts in the client's folder.

  • TazUOv26.0923.64 or later
  • Razor EnhancedWindows
  • ClassicUO web clientpaste a scan

Import

Clients that write scan files are picked up by themselves. The web client pastes its scan instead.

Settings and themes

Default is the clean look. Britannia, the one on this page, dresses Pack Rat in parchment and brass. Light, dark or follow your computer.

Download
